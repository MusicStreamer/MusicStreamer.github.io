Music Player

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

The short version: Music Player stores your library and preferences primarily on your device. If you purchase iCloud Sync, selected library information is also stored in your private iCloud database. The App uses third-party services to provide music search and playback, advertising, analytics, diagnostics, remote configuration, and purchases.

This policy explains what information is handled, why it is used, when it is shared, and the choices available to you.

1. Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

Music Player (the “App”) is operated by Satish Maleku (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy applies to the App, its related support communications, and this privacy-policy webpage.

The App does not require you to provide a name, email address, or traditional user account to listen to music. Some providers may nevertheless process device, network, usage, purchase, or diagnostic information as described below.

Information stored locally

Your playlists, favorites, recently played history, recent searches, preferences, and local entitlement flags are stored on your device using the App’s local database and system preferences. We do not routinely receive the contents of this local storage.

Optional iCloud Sync and backup files

If you purchase and use iCloud Sync, the App stores a library snapshot in your private Apple CloudKit database. The snapshot can contain track identifiers, titles, artists, favorite dates, playlist names, playlist track identifiers, creation and modification dates, and records needed to synchronize deletions. It does not contain actual audio files or playable stream URLs.

Private CloudKit data belongs to the signed-in iCloud user, counts against that user’s iCloud storage, and is private by default. Apple processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. If you manually export a backup file, you control where that copy is saved or shared.

In-app purchases

The App offers non-consumable purchases for ad-free listening, iCloud Sync, or a bundle of those features. Purchases and restorations are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your Apple Account and payment credentials. We do not receive or store your payment-card number, bank details, or billing address.

The App receives product and transaction status from Apple and stores local flags showing which features are unlocked. Firebase Analytics may receive events indicating that a purchase screen was viewed, a product identifier and displayed price, or that a purchase or restoration succeeded. Purchase terms are governed by the Apple Standard End User License Agreement and applicable App Store terms.

3. Advertising and Your Choices

The free version of the App uses AppLovin MAX and may use Meta Audience Network, InMobi, and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) to show advertisements. These providers may process device and app information, IP-derived approximate location, advertising identifiers, consent signals, and ad interactions. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

  • Where required, the App requests advertising consent using InMobi Choice CMP.
  • On supported versions of iOS, the App requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing data used to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • You can change tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device.
  • You may contact us to ask about withdrawing advertising consent or available provider-specific choices.
  • Purchasing Ad-Free disables the App’s ad placements, but does not erase information already retained by an advertising provider under its policy.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising providers may process or share information for targeted advertising where allowed by law and by your consent or device settings.

4. Firebase, Google, and YouTube

The App uses Firebase services, including anonymous authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, and Messaging. These services may process an anonymous account or installation identifier, IP address, device and app information, usage events, crash diagnostics, and notification tokens. Firestore provides charts, releases, artist data, playlists, and remote configuration used by the App.

Search terms, autocomplete text, video or playlist identifiers, and playback requests are sent to Google and YouTube services when you use the relevant features. Google may process this information together with network and device information under its own policy, including when you use Google services without signing in.

5. Why We Use Information

We and our providers use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide search, playback, playlists, favorites, purchases, restoration, iCloud Sync, and other requested features.
  • Remember your settings and maintain the App’s functionality across launches.
  • Serve and measure advertising and respect applicable consent choices.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud or abuse, diagnose errors, and improve reliability.
  • Understand aggregate feature performance and purchase-screen effectiveness.
  • Respond to support or privacy requests and comply with legal obligations.

If the App or its operations are transferred, relevant information may be transferred subject to this policy and applicable law.

7. Retention and Deletion

  • Local App data: remains until you clear it using available App controls or remove the App. The App provides controls for recent searches, listening history, favorites, and playlists.
  • iCloud data: remains in your private iCloud database until removed through Apple’s iCloud storage controls. Removing the App from one device may not remove its iCloud data. Synchronization deletion records may remain in the library snapshot for up to 90 days so deletions can propagate to other devices.
  • Purchases: local entitlement flags remain until App data is removed. Apple separately retains App Store purchase history under its policies so eligible purchases can be restored.
  • Provider data: Firebase, Google, AppLovin, Meta, InMobi, Liftoff, Apple, and GitHub retain information according to their policies and legal obligations.
  • Support messages: are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

To ask about access, correction, deletion, or consent withdrawal, contact us using the address below. Because the App does not use a traditional account, we may be unable to identify provider-held information without an identifier or additional details, and some requests may need to be directed to the relevant provider.

8. Security and International Processing

We use platform security features and encrypted network connections where supported. CloudKit protects private data through the user’s Apple Account and Apple’s security controls. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States. Their privacy policies describe their safeguards and processing locations.
